Subcontractors

"Similarly situated" = holds the same small-business status the contract was set aside for and is small under the contract's NAICS code — verified, not assumed. Its own lower-tier subcontracting still counts: add that as a separate line marked "counts".

This tool is an arithmetic aid, not legal advice and not a compliance determination. The clause in your awarded contract, 13 CFR 125.6 as in force, and your contracting officer govern. Mixed contracts follow the principal-purpose rule. Verify every similarly situated status with evidence.

How the number is computed

Counted subcontracting = payments to subcontractors that are not similarly situated (minus materials, on supply and construction contracts). Percentage = counted subcontracting ÷ amount paid by the government. It is compared with the limit for the contract type: 50% services, 50% supplies, 85% general construction, 75% special trade construction.
