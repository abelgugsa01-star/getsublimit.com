SubLimit — Your first report
Narrated walkthrough with fictional practice records

00:00 — Before you start
Before the walkthrough, here is how you get SubLimit. On getsublimit.com, click Get started.

00:08 — Before you start
Download the Windows installer from the start page and run it once. A SubLimit icon appears on your desktop.

00:17 — Before you start
The first time it opens, read the one-time agreement, tick the box, and click I agree.

00:24 — Before you start
Then sign in with your business email. SubLimit emails you a code; enter it, and checkout opens in your browser. Thirty days free, then one hundred forty-nine dollars a month until you cancel. Your subscription covers two Windows computers. Now the walkthrough begins.


00:45 — Getting started
Welcome to SubLimit. This walkthrough uses real application screens and fictional practice records. Pause whenever you want to follow along.

00:55 — Open your practice contract
Download and extract the practice files from the tutorial page. In a separate practice workspace, choose Open configuration and select contract dot JSON.

01:06 — Review your contract
Contract setup contains the reporting month, contract category, and program details. The sample is already filled in. Your own contract needs its own verified facts.

01:18 — Confirm applicability
Confirm whether the FAR clause applies, and record the supporting evidence. If you are unsure, leave the answer blank and seek clarification.

01:28 — Review people and vendors
Next, review People and vendors. Use stable identities and documented subcontractor status. A similar name does not establish identity or similarly situated status.

01:40 — Check government receipts
Government receipts supplies the payment basis. In this fictional example, eligible government payments total one hundred thousand dollars. Payroll cost is a separate measure.

01:52 — Select the payroll workbook
Open Import and mapping. Under payroll and timekeeping, choose Select CSV or XLSX files, and select payroll dot XLSX from the practice folder.

02:05 — Review the saved mapping
The sample includes saved column mappings. Check the worksheet name, header row, date format, and exact column headings before importing your own files.

02:15 — Preview the source headings
Choose Preview first file to see the original headings and rows. Check the worker identifiers, job numbers, dates, and hours against your mapping.

02:25 — Select subcontractor payments
Scroll down to Subcontractor payments and time. Select subcontractors dot XLSX in this section, and check its mapping too.

02:34 — Import and calculate
When both files and mappings are ready, choose Import and calculate. SubLimit processes the reporting files locally on your computer.

02:44 — Read the counted amount
The sample result counts eighteen thousand four hundred twenty dollars of subcontracting.

02:50 — Read the payment basis
The eligible government payment basis is one hundred thousand dollars.

02:55 — Check the percentage
Dividing the counted amount by that payment basis gives eighteen point four two percent. The sample is compliant against its configured fifty percent limit.

03:06 — Trace a contribution
To examine the evidence, double click a payment contribution. Here we open the first row.

03:13 — Inspect the original source
The detail shows the source worksheet, row number, field, and original value. Use this trace to reconcile a result with the spreadsheet that produced it.

03:23 — Practice a missing fact
Now let us try an incomplete fact. For this exercise only, leave the contract applicability answer blank, then import and calculate again.

03:33 — Review required
SubLimit now shows Review required and leaves the calculation unavailable. It does not guess that the clause applies.

03:42 — Resolve the cause
Open Exceptions to see what needs attention. Use Edit contract facts to correct the confirmed facts, then calculate again. Obtain evidence before changing an unknown answer.

03:54 — Prepare the workforce draft
The OCC workforce report is a separate workflow. Confirm that your contract requires this form revision. Its personnel and hours are separate from the FAR payment calculation.

04:07 — Load practice staffing
After restoring the sample confirmation and recalculating, choose Import staffing file and open staffing dot JSON from the practice folder.

04:17 — Review the worker lines
This example has ten worker lines, with all imported hours reconciled. Review the workers, hours, and contractor facts before preparing the draft.

04:27 — Save and export the draft
Save the staffing draft to keep it with workspace backups. Use Export unsigned Word draft to prepare the document. Inspect every page, including continuation pages.

04:39 — Officer review is required
Your authorized officer remains responsible for reviewing, certifying, and submitting the report. The application prepares the draft and does not sign on your behalf.

04:50 — Export your reporting package
Back in Results, Export package lets you save the reporting output. Store exported files securely, along with the evidence your organization needs.

05:01 — Revisit reporting history
History preserves earlier runs, including the incomplete example. Select a run and choose Open selected run to revisit it.

05:10 — Protect and recover your work
In Backup and restore, create a local backup and test restoring into a new folder. For business records, use a protected workspace and keep its passphrase safely.

05:21 — Keep your records safe
SubLimit cannot recover a forgotten password. Original input files and exported reports need their own protection. Keep practice files separate from business records.

05:33 — Your first report: complete
You have now seen how to import, check, trace, and preserve a reporting period. Use the written walkthrough and practice files on the tutorial page to try it yourself.
